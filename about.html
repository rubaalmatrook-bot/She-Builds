About She Builds

A wider perspective

She Builds celebrates female architects from different countries and cultures. It introduces their backgrounds, ideas and projects in concise, approachable language for curious visitors of any age or background.

One story each day

Daily rotation is planned for a later step and is not active. The future daily feature will be selected from a finite, curated collection using the current UTC calendar date. Everyone sees the same feature on the same UTC date. Once all profiles have appeared, the cycle repeats; the archive does not grow automatically.
