About She Builds

Architecture,
through her eyes.

Discover the women shaping the spaces around us.

A wider perspective

She Builds celebrates female architects from different countries and cultures. It introduces their backgrounds, ideas and projects in concise, approachable language for curious visitors of any age or background.

One story each day

The daily feature is selected from a finite, curated collection using the current UTC calendar date. Everyone sees the same feature on the same UTC date. Once all profiles have appeared, the cycle repeats; the archive does not grow automatically.

The Archive now includes seven selected architects. Each profile includes verified stories and three photographed projects or design works.

Sources & image credits

Biographical facts, projects and interesting details require factual sources before publication. Sources are linked alongside the verified information in each Archive profile.

All seven architects have portraits and three featured works with photographs, photographer credits, source links and reuse-license information. The local typographic share artwork was created for She Builds. Typography: Cormorant Garamond and Libre Baskerville, served by Google Fonts, with local serif fallbacks.
